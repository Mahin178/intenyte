FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you order.

How do I order an NT-01?

Fill in the NT-01 order form with your details. Our team will contact you to confirm your order, delivery, and payment — no online payment required upfront.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available — you pay only when the NT-01 reaches your door.

What warranty does the NT-01 have?

Every NT-01 comes with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. See the full warranty details.

Does the NT-01 need an internet connection?

No. The NT-01 works over Wi-Fi for smart features like live weather and scheduling, and also supports full offline control of lights and fans.

What can the NT-01 control?

Lights and fans with precise linear speed control, four theme colours, plus live weather and clock displays on its dual touch screens.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is arranged after our team confirms your order by call, so you'll know the exact timeline for your location before you pay.

Where can I get the INTENYTE app?

Download it directly from our app download page — free for Android, installed in under a minute.

Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us.

When you place an order or contact us, we collect only the information needed to fulfil your request — your name, email, phone number, delivery address, and any message you send.

We use this information to process orders, arrange delivery, and respond to your questions. We do not sell your personal data. Order and contact forms are processed by Formspree on our behalf, and we use the Meta Pixel to measure site visits and ad performance. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies, and the Meta Pixel may set cookies in your browser.

To ask about or delete your data, simply contact us and we'll take care of it.
